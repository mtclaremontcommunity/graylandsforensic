On the parliamentary record

Questions on Notice tracker

A Question on Notice puts a question to a Minister formally, in writing, on the record of WA Parliament — and requires a written answer, also on the record. Some questions instead get asked and answered on the spot in the Chamber, as a Question Without Notice; we track those here too, and mark them as such. This page follows every one lodged about the Graylands Reconfiguration and Forensics Project, as it happens, in one place.

Unlike a letter to a Minister's office, a Question on Notice can't simply go unanswered — it sits on the Notice Paper until it is. That makes it one of the few tools available to a local MP that compels a written, attributable response on a fixed timeframe. This page is not a substitute for the Correspondence page — it tracks a different, parallel channel: formal parliamentary questions, not direct letters.
