Where we stand

We support forensic mental health services being properly resourced in Western Australia. These patients need treatment and WA needs the capacity. Our question is whether anyone has established that this model is safe in this location — not whether the patients deserve care.

Three questions still unanswered

These sit behind every concern on this site.

  1. Infrastructure WA found the business case alone had insufficient information for an investment decision. What further work was completed before it was funded three weeks later?
  2. Infrastructure WA says 20 sites were assessed. Why was Graylands selected, and will the full comparison — including lower-density sites or locations closer to prisons — be released?
  3. Where is the location-specific risk assessment for community integration, including supervised and unsupervised leave near schools, streets and public spaces?
