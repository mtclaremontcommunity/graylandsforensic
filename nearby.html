Will neighbourhoods around Graylands Hospital still feel safe?

A major shift from general psychiatric care to a forensic mental health campus — surrounded by homes and schools. No safety plan for this location has ever been published, and Graylands' own record shows why that matters.

What does "forensic mental health" actually mean?

What is a forensic bed?

Forensic beds are for high-security patients who pose a significant risk to themselves or others and need secure detention alongside specialist psychiatric treatment. They can include people accused of, convicted of, or found to have committed very serious offences — including homicide and child sexual offences.

Why does the CLMI Act matter?

The Criminal Law (Mental Impairment) Act 2023 means forensic patients aren't confined inside a secure hospital indefinitely. Depending on their circumstances and approvals, they progress through stages of treatment that include supervised and unsupervised leave in the community — walking the same streets as everyone else in the neighbourhood. That's why location matters, and why this campaign is asking whether proximity to homes, schools, streets and public spaces was properly assessed before Graylands was selected.

Where we stand

We support forensic mental health services being properly resourced in Western Australia. These patients need treatment and WA needs the capacity. Our question is whether anyone has established that this model is safe in this location — not whether the patients deserve care.

Key questions still unanswered

  • Infrastructure WA found the business case alone had insufficient information for an investment decision. What further work was completed before approval?
  • Infrastructure WA says 20 sites were assessed. Why was Graylands selected — and will the full comparison, including lower-density sites or locations closer to prisons, be released?
  • Where is the location-specific risk assessment for community integration, including supervised and unsupervised leave near schools, streets and public spaces?

We can't prove this model isn't safe here. The government hasn't shown that it is.
