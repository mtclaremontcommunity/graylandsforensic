Step 2, in full

Share your story

A signature count is easy for a Minister's office to wave away. A specific account from a named, contactable resident is not. If something about this — the site, the process, a safety concern, what it would mean for your family, your street, or your work — is worth saying in your own words, this is the place.

Leave your email if you're open to us following up to verify details, or if part of what you've written might be worth quoting — always with your OK asked first, never assumed. Anonymous is completely fine too; we just won't be able to follow up or use it directly if you go that way.

What this is used for, and what it isn't.

  • If you've told us about an experience with Graylands, we'll only ever quote it anonymously, and only after checking back with you first.
  • An email address is only ever used to follow up on what you've written — never published, never shared outside the small group of volunteers running this campaign, never used for anything beyond it.
  • Nothing here is published automatically. If it checks out and you're open to it, it may be added to the Safety risks log — only ever anonymously, and only with your OK.
  • Want anything you've shared removed? Email mtclaremontcommunity@gmail.com.
  • This is run by a volunteer resident group, not a registered organisation — we're handling what you share with the same care a formal one would, and wanted you to know exactly who's holding it.
